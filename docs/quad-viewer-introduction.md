**Four FPV feeds. One ultra-bright screen. Built for race day.**

The **HDZero Quad Viewer** combines the functionality of four HDZero monitors into a single, rugged unit with a large  **10.1-inch high-brightness display,** making it ideal for **race spotters, spectators, and race directors** who need to track multiple pilots at once.

With support for  **HDZero digital and analog video** , the Quad Viewer allows you to monitor up to **four FPV feeds simultaneously** in a quad-view layout or switch to a **single full-screen feed** for detailed viewing.

**Designed for Outdoor Events**

The Quad Viewer features a  **super-bright 1800+ nit screen** , ensuring excellent visibility even in direct sunlight. Whether you are standing trackside or managing an event tent, the display stays clear and readable all day long.

**Smart Video Handling**

Each channel automatically switches between **HDZero digital and analog video** based on signal strength for the selected race channel. This makes the Quad Viewer compatible with mixed pilot setups without manual intervention.

**Ideal for Racing and Events**

* **Spotters** can track pilot position and relative progress in real time
* **Spectators** get a clear view of multiple pilots simultaneously
* **Race directors** benefit from a built-in monitoring screen and simplified HDMI output

The Quad Viewer also functions as a  **4-to-1 HDMI multiplexer**, combining all four FPV feeds into a single HDMI output in a quad-grid layout for externaldisplays or streaming setups.

**Note**: HDMI output resolution is limited to  **1280×720p** . For higher-resolution per-channel output, the standard **HDZero Event VRX** remains the preferred solution.

**Flexible Power Options**

Power the Quad Viewer easily using:

* XT60 input (2S-6S LiPo) - perfect for race-day battery swaps
* DC barrel connector - for bench or fixed installations
* USB-C - great for powering with a USB-C PD power bank

Finding power at the field is never a problem.

**Key Features**

* Quad-view display of 4 simultaneous FPV feeds
* Switchable quad-view or single full-screen mode
* Supports HDZero digital and analog video
* Automatic digital/analog switching per channel
* Ultra-bright 10.1-inch outdoor-readable display
* Built-in 4-channel HDMI output (720p)
* Portable, all-in-one race monitoring solution
* Built-in DVR (record only, no playback)

**Specifications**

* Screen Size: 10.1 inches
* Brightness: 1800nit+
* Screen Resolution: 1280×720 @ 60fps
* HDMI Output Resolution: 1280×720 @ 60fps
* Dimensions: 260 × 180 × 25 mm
* Weight: 810 g
* Power Input: XT60 (2-6S) or DC barrel connector
